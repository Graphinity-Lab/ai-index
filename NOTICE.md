## Upstream Data Providers & Research Consortiums
All trademarks, product names, model weights, benchmark suites, and organization logos belong to their respective owners and are cited strictly for factual, educational, and comparative telemetry:

- **OpenRouter API** (`https://openrouter.ai`) — Live model catalog, context windows, and per-token API pricing.
- **Hugging Face Open LLM Leaderboard** (`https://huggingface.co/open-llm-leaderboard`) — Open-weight MMLU-Pro and GPQA evaluations.
- **LiveBench** (`https://livebench.ai`) — Contamination-free monthly benchmark evaluations.
- **LMSYS / LMArena** (`https://lmarena.ai`) — Blind human preference Text & WebDev Elo ratings.
- **Artificial Analysis** (`https://artificialanalysis.ai`) — Throughput (tokens/sec), TTFT latency, and cost-per-task telemetry.
- **Epoch AI** (`https://epoch.ai`) — Training compute (FLOPs), training cost estimates, and scaling-law benchmarks.
- **International Energy Agency (IEA) & Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (LBNL)** — Global and US data center electricity consumption and cooling water intensity benchmarks.
- **TrendForce** — DRAM/HBM wafer allocation, NAND SSD, and PC/server BOM inflation indices.
- **Yahoo Finance & Public SEC Filings** — Public equity quotes, CapEx disclosures, and financial run-rates.
